## How My Experience Connects

My SRE background shapes how I approach architecture. I do not treat cloud design as a diagram-only exercise: every solution must be observable, recoverable, secure, operable, and cost-aware.

- **SRE:** observability, incident prevention, capacity, resilience, backup, and recovery.
- **Platform engineering:** Kubernetes, GitOps, CI/CD, security controls, and developer enablement.
- **Infrastructure as code:** Terraform, OpenTofu, Terragrunt, and repeatable environment delivery.
- **AWS architecture:** identity, compute, storage, networking, security, reliability, and cost optimization.

This portal provides evidence of that progression through reproducible labs, implementation guides, architecture patterns, and lessons from hands-on engineering.
