# Career Progression

This portfolio is organized as a progression rather than a disconnected article archive. Each stage builds capabilities required for the next.

## Stage 1: Site Reliability Engineering

The foundation is reliable operations: understand system behavior, establish useful telemetry, plan capacity, protect data, and recover predictably.

Key capabilities include metrics, logs, alerting, operational automation, backup and restore, RPO/RTO planning, resource management, and production troubleshooting.

[Browse SRE and observability guides](setup-monitoring-stack.md)

## Stage 2: Kubernetes & Cloud-Native Engineering

Kubernetes expands the operational scope from servers to distributed platforms. This stage covers cluster access, workload management, ingress, autoscaling, PKI, secrets, and container security.

[Browse Kubernetes guides](k8s-resource-management.md)

## Stage 3: Platform Engineering

Platform engineering turns operational knowledge into reusable capabilities for delivery teams. The focus shifts to GitOps, standardized pipelines, multi-cluster control, registries, policy, and infrastructure modules.

[Browse platform engineering guides](argocd-multiple-deployment.md)

## Stage 4: AWS Solutions Architecture

Solutions architecture broadens the decision space to business requirements, workload characteristics, security, resilience, integration, migration, and cost. My SRE background provides the operational lens; platform engineering provides repeatability; AWS services provide the architecture building blocks.

[Explore the AWS architecture hub](aws-architecture.md)
